01

Warm-up Library

Do 8–10 minutes before every session. Pick 4–5 of these dynamic moves — they raise your heart rate and loosen the joints so you move well and stay injury-free. Aim for ~40 seconds each.

02

The Weekly Plan

Five focused sessions a week. Tap a day, follow the moves, and check each one off as you finish. Log your reps or weight so you can see yourself getting stronger.

03

Cooldown Stretches

Finish every session with 5 minutes of easy stretching. Hold each for 20–30 seconds, breathe, and never bounce. This is how you recover faster and stay flexible.

04

Full-Body Mobility Flow

Breathe deep, move smooth, feel open. A gentle flow to move through on rest days or after training — flow slowly from one pose to the next, spending a few breaths in each.

Tips
  • Breathe slowly through each position.
  • Let gravity deepen the stretch \u2014 don't push.
  • Stay relaxed, and never force your range.
